Know who you are dealing with, before you deal with them.

BaseLens reads any wallet or contract on Base and gives back a plain-English risk read. It is built for AI agents, which now move money onchain and need to check an address before they trust it.

View the source How it works
How it works

One paid call, one clear answer

No account, no API key. The caller pays a few cents of USDC per lookup, the way agents are starting to pay for everything.

1

Send an address

An agent or a person posts a Base address to the endpoint.

2

Pay a few cents

The endpoint asks for a tiny USDC payment over x402. The caller's wallet pays it automatically.

3

Get the read

BaseLens pulls the onchain history and returns a structured risk and activity summary it can act on.

What you get back

A structured read, not a chatbot reply

{
  "address": "0x4200...0006",
  "selfLabel": "WETH (canonical)",
  "riskLevel": "low",
  "activitySummary": "Canonical Wrapped Ether on Base. High volume,
     interacts with major routers and the L2 bridge.",
  "riskFactors": [],
  "recommendation": "Safe to interact with."
}
Under the hood

Real onchain depth, grounded risk

It reads native and token transfers, labels known contracts, computes risk signals from the data, then has Claude write the read on top of those facts. Open source.
